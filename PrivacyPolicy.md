# Privacy Policy - GLB (Generic Leveling Bot)
> Last Updated: July 29 2026
> Support Server: [https://discord.gg/sPQFM9Rj33](https://discord.gg/sPQFM9Rj33)

GLB respects your privacy and is committed to being transparent about the data we process.

By using the bot, you agree to the terms outlined in this policy.

---

## 1. Data Collection
To provide the bots feature we store following data:
* **User Identifiers:** Discord User IDs (to link data to your account).
* **Level Data:** Experience, Level and Date joined for each server you use this bot on.

---

## 2. Data Storage & Usage

**Storage**
All user data is stored locally using **Sqlite3**. This data is kept on our private hosting infrastructure and is not accessible to unauthorized third parties.

---

## 3. Sharing & Trhird Parties
We do **not** sell, trade or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes. Data is only accessed by the bot's developers for maintenance or shared if strictly required by law enforcement or to comply with Discord's Terms of Service.

---

## 4. Removal & Retention

**Your Rights**
Users can delete their own data at any time using the /delete-my-data command.
> **Note:** The command is still yet to be implemented, so please be patient.

**Retention**
Data can only be removed by the user and will also not be deleted after a guilds deletion.

---

## 5. Policy Changes
This privacy policy may be updated at any time to reflect bot changes, feature additions, or legal requirements. Updates will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
